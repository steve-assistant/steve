# Section 1 of docs/plan/07-voice.md: the press to a live microphone at most
# 150 ms, with the tick once it is live; the release to the first word at
# most 2.5 s at P90, with OpenAI as fast as it is faked (keys.HEAR_S).
scenario ptt-ask-and-answer
about hold the key, ask the weather, let go, hear the answer
length 10
reply /weather/ 0.5: It is 64 and clear today, with no rain expected.
0.5    key: press
+0.1   user: What is the weather today?
+0.3   key!: release
expect heard ^what is the weather today
expect asked weather
expect transcribed == 1
expect mic-live <= 0.15
expect tick-live
expect first-audio <= 2.5
