# A press while the last reply is still being written: the microphone
# closes on the release. Closed by the turn, which waits for the one before
# it, it stayed open seconds after the release, and what the room said then
# went with the request (review of steps 7 to 9, findings 1 and 31).
scenario ptt-press-while-writing
about a press while the last reply is still being written closes the microphone on its release
length 12
reply /weather/ 3.0: It is 64 and clear today.
reply /what time/ 0.3: It is four o clock.
0.5    key: press
+0.1   user: What is the weather today?
+0.3   key: release
+0.5   key: press
+0.1   user: What time is it?
+0.3   key!: release
+0.2   user: Never mind the weather.
expect mic-shut <= 0.1
expect heard ^what time is it\?$
expect asked what time
expect tick-live
