# A press during a reply stops it at once, and starts a new capture: the
# bar is silence within 0.15 s of the press.
scenario ptt-press-over-a-reply
about a press during a long answer silences it, and the new request is heard
length 14
reply /history of tea/ 0.5: Tea was first drunk in China, as a medicine. It spread
  along the trade routes to Japan and Korea. Merchants brought it to Europe in
  the sixteenth century, and it became a daily habit in Britain.
reply /what time/ 0.3: It is four o clock.
0.5        key: press
+0.1       user: Tell me about the history of tea.
+0.3       key: release
voice+1.5  key!: press
+0.1       user: What time is it?
+0.3       key: release
expect silenced <= 0.15
expect heard ^what time is it
expect asked what time
